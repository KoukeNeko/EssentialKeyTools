## New

- **Next track** and **Previous track** actions, so a gesture can skip through what you are listening to without unlocking the phone. Requested in [#1](https://github.com/KoukeNeko/EssentialKeyTools/issues/1).
- A **Diagnostics** screen showing the last crash the app caught, alongside the process exits Android itself recorded. Its **Report on GitHub** button opens a prefilled issue and copies the full report, so reporting a crash is a paste rather than a transcription.
- A shortcut to the system Do Not Disturb access screen, which the ringer cycle needs before it can reach silent mode.

## Fixed

- The ringer-cycle action crashed when it ran while the phone was already silent, because the fallback path retried a change the platform had just refused.
- The action picker crashed when an installed app reported an oversized launcher icon.

## Privacy

Crash reports are written to the app's private storage on your device and are never transmitted. There is no crash-reporting service and no server to send them to. A report leaves your device only if you copy or share it yourself, and it deliberately contains nothing the accessibility service observes — no screen content, no notification text, and no record of which keys you pressed.

The copied report is marked sensitive so Android keeps it out of the clipboard preview, and the accessibility and privacy wording now describes the report precisely.

Verified on a Nothing Phone (3) before release.
