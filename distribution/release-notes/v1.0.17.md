## New features & improvements

🎛️ Added a **Custom** haptic level with its own page. Pick **Custom** under Haptic feedback, then **Edit pattern** to set the number of pulses (1–10), pulse length (5–200 ms), frequency (up to 25 Hz) and amplitude (10–100%). Each slider plays the result when you let go. A phone that cannot vary vibration strength, like the Phone (3), approximates amplitude by switching the motor on and off rapidly. Requested in [#4](https://github.com/KoukeNeko/EssentialKeyTools/issues/4).

📳 Added **Vibrate only when an action runs** under the Haptic feedback levels. A double or triple press vibrates once when its action runs, a long press vibrates when it triggers, and a gesture set to None never vibrates. A single press waits for the double-press window to close first when a double or triple press is also mapped. Requested in [#4](https://github.com/KoukeNeko/EssentialKeyTools/issues/4).

🔓 The unlock wizard can now disable and restore Essential Space and Recorder through [Shizuku](https://shizuku.rikka.app/), for builds where App Info greys out the Disable button. It needs Shizuku running and permission granted to this app; the App Info path is still there. Requested in [#6](https://github.com/KoukeNeko/EssentialKeyTools/issues/6).

## Bug fixes

📳 Light, Medium and Strong haptic feedback now feel different from each other. On the Phone (3) all three used to produce the same buzz, and on the Phone (4a) Medium did nothing at all, because those vibrators do not support the system's built-in click effects. Each level is now an explicit pulse: Light 15 ms, Medium 50 ms, Strong 80 ms. Reported in [#4](https://github.com/KoukeNeko/EssentialKeyTools/issues/4).
